# Green — vegetable rows and date palms → soil detail (storyboard 08)

Start frame = `@farm_rows` (the cabbage rows / date palm still). Image-to-video, 5 s, 16:9, 1080p.

```
5-second 16:9 shot, one continuous camera move: a low dolly glides forward along the rows of a desert vegetable farm toward the date palms at sunrise, then sinks and tilts down onto the soil at the base of a plant.

REFERENCES
@farm_rows — THE VERY FIRST FRAME OF THE CLIP. Take the farm exactly: the long rows of cabbage converging toward the horizon at frame-left, the pale sand furrows between them, the raised sand bank, the grove of date palms at frame-right, the low sun glowing through the palms at top-right. Every frame of the clip is inside this same farm.

LOOK
24mm, camera low, about 60 cm above the sand. Clean modern digital cinema look, deep clean shadows, rich healthy green in the leaves, warm golden sunrise light from the top-right, long soft shadows across the furrows, light haze over the far palms only. Never washed out, never teal.

SHOTS
0.0–3.2s — Exactly @farm_rows. A slow, steady dolly straight forward down the sand furrow between the two nearest rows, toward the vanishing point at frame-left. The cabbages stream past the lower edges of the frame with strong low-angle parallax; the date palms at frame-right grow slowly and drift toward the edge of frame. Leaves stir faintly in a light breeze; palm fronds sway gently; fine dust glints in the sunbeams through the palms. The camera never stops.
3.2–5.0s — Without slowing, the camera sinks lower and tilts down toward the soil at the base of the next cabbage on the right of the furrow. The horizon and the palms leave the top of the frame. The frame ends on soil detail: damp dark sand around the root, a drip-irrigation line, the lowest leaves edging the frame, sunlight raking across the grains. Still drifting forward on the last frame.

SOUND
No music, no voice. Soft desert-morning wind, a light rustle of leaves, a few distant birds.

KEEP CONSISTENT
Opens on exactly @farm_rows.
One unbroken move: dolly forward, then sink and tilt down. No cut, no stop.
The rows stay straight and parallel; plants never move position or multiply.
The palms keep their places; only their fronds sway.
No people, no hands, no animals, no vehicles, no tractor, no buildings.
Greens stay rich and natural; sand stays warm pale gold.
No text, no logos.
```
